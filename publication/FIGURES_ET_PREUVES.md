## Légendes de travail en anglais

**Figure 1.** Four-application structure of the BYTE NDT workflow. Each examination-level application is paired with a blade-and-scanner context application. Source UT data remain case-specific; the mechanical layer adds assembly and motion context.

**Figure 5.** C2 scanner application accepted in the digital review at the beginning, intermediate position and end of the registered path. The central blade geometry is unchanged. Shell opacity and adjacent-blade transparency are visibility settings. The images illustrate assembly and motion; they do not establish manufacturing clearance or physical coupling.

**Figure 6.** Source cartographies for C1 and C2. Units and references must be stated separately for each map. The calculated coherent-sensitivity display is not a measured-pressure map; frozen examination-table maxima are distinguished from encoded-signal candidate outputs.
